Introduction to the edited volume, written with Maya Avis and Maria Sapignoli. Data generated by surveillance technologies increasingly shapes how states govern, and automated decision-making systems promise to bring a new "rationality" to the bureaucracies of criminal justice systems. The introduction sets out the ethnographic perspective the volume takes on these developments.
