Area-based predictive policing, variously described as AI, machine learning or big data, has received significant public attention. This chapter draws on ethnographic interviews with technology leads in UK and US police forces and with companies developing predictive policing products, as well as visits to industry events.

It looks at the often invisible "infrastructure work" of iterative innovation and technical maintenance. As police data is increasingly hosted on cloud services, the chapter questions the role private companies play in the ongoing normative decisions bound up with the infrastructure they provide.
